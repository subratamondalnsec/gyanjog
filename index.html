About Gyanjog Group • Est. Under Act of 1882

Welcome to Gyanjog Group

Pioneering Excellence in Psychology, Counselling & Spiritual Mind Sciences

Gyanjog Group is a nationally recognized educational and training organization dedicated to excellence in mental health sciences, psychological counselling, and holistic spiritual development. Functioning under the Indian Trusts Act of 1882, we operate two specialized autonomous institutions with clear academic mandates to guarantee authentic certification, clinical credibility, and professional excellence across India and Bangladesh.
