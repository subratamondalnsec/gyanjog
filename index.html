Welcome to Gyanjog Group

Pioneering Excellence in Psychology, Counselling & Spiritual Mind Sciences

Gyanjog Group is a nationally recognized educational and training organization dedicated to excellence in mental health sciences, psychological counselling, and holistic spiritual development. Operating under the Indian Trusts Act of 1882, we have established two specialized autonomous institutions with clear academic mandates to ensure authentic certification, academic credibility, and global relevance.
