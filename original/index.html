welcome to Gyanjog Group


Gyanjog Group is a nationally recognized educational and training organization dedicated to excellence in Psychology, Counselling, Mind Sciences, and Spiritual Development. Under the Gyanjog Group, two specialized institutions are actively functioning:

  • Genius Mind Brain Development Academy
  • Gyanjog Institute of Psychology and Spiritual Development

Each institution operates with a clear academic focus to ensure authentic certification, academic credibility, and professional excellence.

Our Academic Structure & Certification Authority
All Psychology, Counselling, and Mental Health–related courses are certified by Genius Mind Brain Development Academy. All Spiritual Science, Mind Training, and Consciousness-based courses are certified by Gyanjog Institute of Psychology and Spiritual Development. This structured division ensures clarity, specialization, and global relevance of our certifications.

Courses We Offer

  • We currently offer a wide range of professional and career-oriented programs, including:
  • Child Counselling Certificate Course
  • Diploma in Child Counselling
  • Career Counselling Program
  • Neuro Linguistic Programming (NLP) Training
  • Residential Training Program (Therapy & Mind Training)

Our programs are designed for psychologists, counsellors, teachers, parents, professionals, and aspiring mental health practitioners.
Legal Status & Government Recognition

  • Gyanjog Group of Organizations is:
  • Established under the Act of 1882
  • Registered under the Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi
  • Registered under the Government of West Bengal

We are proudly:

  • Listed with NITI Aayog, Government of India
  • Registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Government of India
  • Affiliated with National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC), Government of India
  • Affiliated with Glocal University
  • Recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC)
  • These affiliations reflect our strong compliance, transparency, and institutional credibility.

Our Reach & Impact

  • 9 Schools are currently associated with our organization
  • 3,500+ students are receiving professional training under Gyanjog Group
  • 1,500+ professional learners are currently enrolled in our Online Child Counselling Programs
  • Active presence in India and Bangladesh, providing online Child Counselling training

Our Vision
Our mission is to create highly skilled, ethically grounded, and socially responsible professionals in the fields of psychology, counselling, education, and spiritual sciences—blending scientific knowledge with human values.
